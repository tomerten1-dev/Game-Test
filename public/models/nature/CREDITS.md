# Nature models

Trees (CommonTree_1, CommonTree_3), flowering bush (Bush_Common_Flowers), clovers (Clover_1, Clover_2) and textures
(leaves_tree, leaves_white, flowers, bark_normal) are from **Quaternius – Stylized Nature** (https://quaternius.com), **CC0 1.0**.

Converted from FBX to GLB (meters, one mesh per material) and textures resized to web-friendly sizes.
The bark color texture was not included, so bark uses a flat brown color with the bark normal map and baked vertex AO.
