# KayKit models

All models in `kk/` and `../chars/` are by **Kay Lousberg** (https://kaylousberg.com), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain).

| Folder | Pack | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kk/` buildings, trees, rocks, clouds, props | KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Medieval-Hexagon-Pack-1.0 |
| `kk/city_*` buildings, roads, cars, street lights, benches, hydrants, trash, dumpsters, water tower | KayKit City Builder Bits 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-City-Builder-Bits-1.0 |
| `kk/furn_*` café tables & chairs, armchairs, couches, lamps, potted cacti | KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Furniture-Bits-1.0 |
| `kk/chest_gold` | KayKit Dungeon Remastered 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Dungeon-Remastered-1.0 |
| `chars/` Knight, Barbarian, Mage, Rogue, Rogue_Hooded + animations | KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 | https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0 |

Converted from glTF to self-contained GLB. The characters had their default held weapons removed and the
animations moved into one shared `chars/anims.glb` (only the clips the game uses) to keep downloads small.
