# Trees

From the **Stylized Trees Pack** by Elijah Cobden: https://elijahcobden.itch.io/stylized-trees-pack
Provided by the project owner. Check the pack page for its license terms before redistributing.

Converted from FBX to GLB (meters, +Y up, base at y = 0, one mesh per material) with the pack's
trunk and leaf textures embedded as 512 px WebP. Leaf textures are tinted per tree in the game.

| Files | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| STOak1-3, STColumnar1, STColumnar3 | Detailed leafy trees shown near the camera |
| STPine1-4 | Detailed pines shown near the camera |
| STWillow1-3 | Willows around Lazy Lake and low ground by the coast |
| STSwiggly1-4 | Small clusters in the meadows |
| STPine5-7, STOak5, STColumnar5 | Dead trees on the mountain, around Rusty Works and on the desert island |
